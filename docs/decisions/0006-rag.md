# 0006 · RAG

**Context.** Users describe problems in their own words ("mi ridate i soldi?", "can I get my money
back?"), the documentation uses its own terms ("Rimborsi", refunds). Semantic embeddings are needed.

**Decision.**
- Embeddings with Titan Text Embeddings V2 on Bedrock, 512 dimensions.
- One chunk per section, prefixed with "Title > Section", where the section is the path of its
  headings ("Notifiche > Notifiche sullo spazio"). Long sections are split at paragraph ends, then at
  sentence ends, then at word boundaries, never within a word. Consecutive chunks share the last
  sentences.
- Next to the vectors, `index_meta.json` records model and dimensions of the embedder. At startup,
  and before loading the index, `check_compatibility` compares them with the configured embedder and
  stops on a mismatch. The region does not count: the same model gives the same vectors in every
  region.
- Vector search filtered by product, `top_k` 5, threshold 0.30 chosen with the retrieval evaluation
  ([0007](0007-evaluation.md)).
- Parallel embedding (`max_concurrency`) with the adaptive retries of botocore on throttling.
- A new index is written in a sibling folder and swapped in only when complete.
- The index is data only: `chunks.json`, `vectors.npy` (loaded with `allow_pickle=False`) and
  `index_meta.json`. It is rebuilt at every run.

**Rejected alternatives.**
- A keyword reranker on top of Titan: in the tests it pushed down the semantically right chunk.
- Incremental ingestion with a manifest: complexity that a few dozen documents do not need.
- Fixed-size character windows: they cut words and sentences, and the chunk loses its meaning.
